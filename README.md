# Guitar Theory Maps

An interactive visual study guide to music theory for guitar, with a piano reference beside every fretboard and clickable sound throughout.

Thirteen chapters, 108 lessons, one page per chapter. Chapters 1 to 10 are the course; 11 to 13 are advanced reference and practice material.

1. Intervals, keys and triads
2. Seventh chords and minor keys
3. Function, progressions and modes
4. Substitution and borrowed chords
5. Chords across the neck
6. Scales and arpeggios across the neck
7. Making music: lines and practice
8. Modal and altered harmony
9. Rhythm and feel
10. Ear training and real songs
11. Advanced voicings
12. Advanced scales and arpeggios
13. Line workshop

Open `index.html` in any browser, or host the folder with GitHub Pages. Keep all the files together in one folder.
